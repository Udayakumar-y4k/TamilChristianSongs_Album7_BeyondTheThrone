This song of deliverance and trust echoes Psalm 3 in full — David’s cry when fleeing from Absalom, surrounded by enemies, yet declaring God as his shield, glory, and lifter of his head. It proclaims the Lord as the Holy Mountain (Zion), arising to save, defeating foes, and blessing all who call on Him. In the eternal day beyond the throne, this prayer finds ultimate fulfillment: no more enemies, no more darkness, only the Lamb’s presence and eternal security (Revelation 21:3–4; 22:3–5).
Closing Prayer for the Listener
Lord Jesus Christ, Holy Mountain and Lifter of our heads,
arise and save us in every trial.
Be our shield and glory when enemies surround.
Let us sleep in peace and wake in Your light.
Deliver us from fear and redeem all who call on You.
In the eternal day, let us dwell in Your presence forever.
Come quickly, and let Your glory shine without end.
