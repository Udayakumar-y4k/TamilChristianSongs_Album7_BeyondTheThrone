Closing Prayer for the Listener
Lord Jesus Christ, God of Righteousness and Light of my salvation,
hear my plea in every distress.
Lift the light of Your face upon me.
Be my shield and glory when enemies rise.
Grant me peaceful sleep and joyful praise.
In the eternal day, let me behold Your face forever.
Come quickly, and let Your glory shine without end.
